About us

A company rooted in Aveyron, looking to the future

ALOLI is an IT services company founded in 2011 in Laguiole, in the heart of Aveyron, southern France. With over 30 years of experience in IT, we support businesses and local authorities in their digital transformation with a human-scale approach.


We develop exclusively with open source technologies: Crystal, Ruby on Rails, Opal, and we host our solutions on FreeBSD servers. This philosophy ensures transparency, sustainability and technological independence for our clients.
